What I'm about.

Who I am

I come from a background in digital media and design, so I live somewhere between creative thinking and logical problem-solving. I enjoy making sense of messy stuff — data, user flows, product goals — and turning it into something that just feels right and works well.

What I've done

I recently finished my M.S. at Carnegie Mellon (HCII · METALS). Before that I was a product designer at Ecovacs, Kwai, and a generative-AI startup, leading 0→1 design across B2B and B2C — from smart cleaning systems to AI data dashboards. Most recently I led UX for Varsity Tutors' parent dashboard.

Why design

I've always loved breaking things down to understand how they work. Design is where I get to connect systems thinking with empathy — solving real problems and making ideas feel intuitive and accessible.

Off the clock

Design competitions, mentoring designers just getting started, traveling everywhere, chasing whales with my Scottie, and serious food hunting.
